# Aggregate Calculator in Pakistan 2026 (NUST, FAST, UET Guide)

If you are a student in Pakistan applying for universities like **NUST, FAST, UET, COMSATS, or LUMS**, then understanding aggregate calculation is very important.

Even a difference of 1% can change your admission result.

---

## 📊 What is Aggregate?

Aggregate is a **final admission score** calculated using:

- Matric marks
- Intermediate marks
- Entry test marks (like NET, ECAT, FAST test)

Universities combine all these marks to decide your merit position.

---

## 🧠 Why Aggregate Matters?

Many students think only entry test is important — but that is wrong.

👉 Your final selection depends on aggregate.

### Example:

- High entry test but low FSC → rejection  
- Medium entry test + good FSC → selection  

---

## 🏫 Common Weightage in Pakistan Universities

Most universities use this formula:

### ✔ Typical Formula:

- Entry Test: **50%**
- Intermediate: **40%**
- Matric: **10%**

⚠️ But every university has slightly different rules.
