# ATS-Friendly Resume Templates 2026: Best CV Templates to Get More Interviews

In 2026, your resume is not just a document — it is the **first filter that decides whether you get an interview or not**.

Most companies now use **ATS (Applicant Tracking System)** to automatically scan and shortlist resumes before a recruiter even sees them.

If your resume is not ATS-friendly, even a highly skilled candidate can get rejected.

---

## What is an ATS-Friendly Resume Template?

An ATS-friendly resume template is a **clean, structured CV format** that can be easily read by software systems used by companies.

It focuses on:

- Simple formatting  
- Proper headings  
- Keyword optimization  
- No complex graphics or tables  

These templates ensure your resume passes ATS filters and reaches HR.

---

## Why ATS-Friendly Templates Matter in 2026

Job competition in 2026 is extremely high.

For a single job post:

- 100+ applicants apply  
- ATS filters remove 70–80% resumes automatically  
- Only optimized resumes reach recruiters  

👉 This means your resume template directly impacts your chances of getting hired.

---

## Features of a Good ATS Resume Template

A professional ATS-friendly resume should include:

### 1. Clean Structure

Clear sections like:

- Header (Name, Contact)  
- Professional Summary  
- Skills  
- Experience  
- Education  
- Projects  

---

### 2. Keyword Optimization

Use job-related keywords such as:

- React.js  
- Node.js  
- JavaScript  
- Problem Solving  
- Communication Skills  

---

### 3. No Heavy Design

Avoid:

- Graphics  
- Icons everywhere  
- Fancy columns  
- Image-based resumes  

ATS systems cannot properly read complex designs.

---

### 4. Simple Fonts

Use clean and readable fonts like:

- Arial  
- Calibri  
- Helvetica

## Example: ATS Resume Structure

A strong ATS resume should follow this format:

### Header
Name | Email | Phone | Location

### Summary
Short 2–3 line introduction about your skills and goals.

### Skills
Programming languages, tools, and expertise.

### Experience
Internships, jobs, freelance work.

### Education
Degree, university name, and year.

### Projects
Real-world projects with clear descriptions.
